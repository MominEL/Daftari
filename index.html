A free notes app for students, with AI built in.

Record a lecture, get notes written from it, and revise from them. Daftari runs on your own computer and costs nothing. Your notes are plain files in a folder you choose.

Download for Windows Download for Mac

Daftari is for Windows and Mac computers. Open this page on your computer to download.

  1. 1 of 5

    Press record

    Record with your microphone, with your computer’s own sound (a Teams call, an online lecture), or both. Daftari saves the audio to disk every 30 seconds, so if the battery dies you lose very little.

  2. 2 of 5

    Read along

    Speech-to-text runs on your computer while you listen, so the lecture is not sent anywhere to be transcribed. Each part shows up in the transcript a few seconds after it is said.

  3. 3 of 5

    Notes appear as you go

    About every ten minutes, the AI you set up writes notes for that part: headings, definitions, formulas. When you press Stop, Daftari puts them together into one page, linked to the full transcript. You read it before anything is saved.

  4. 4 of 5

    Revise from the same page

    Turn the notes into flashcards or a quiz, by hand or with the AI. Spaced repetition shows each card just before you would forget it, and your progress is saved inside the page.

  5. 5 of 5

    Everything stays as plain files

    Every page is one file in a folder you choose: on this computer, or in OneDrive, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox. Back it up, sync it, or read it without Daftari. If you delete the app, your notes are still there.

Why I made this

I’m a student too. I wasn’t going to pay a monthly subscription just to have AI in my notes, and I don’t think other students should have to either. So I made my own notes app, and I’m sharing it.

Daftari is free, and it’s staying free. For the AI part you plug in a key from whichever company you like. A few have free plans, and the app shows you how to get one. Don’t want AI at all? Everything else works without it.

It’s just me working on this, so it’s rough in places. If something breaks or confuses you, please tell me. Honestly, that’s the most helpful thing you can do.

Opening it the first time

Because Daftari is not registered with Microsoft or Apple, Windows and macOS warn you the first time you open it. That is expected. Only continue if you downloaded the file from this page.

Windows

  1. Run the installer.Double-click the file you downloaded.
  2. If “Windows protected your PC” appears,click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac

  1. Drag Daftari into Applications.Open the file you downloaded and drag the icon across.
  2. Open Daftari from Applications.macOS says it can’t check it. Click Done, not “Move to Bin”.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.Scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. Click Open Anyway once more.Type your Mac password. That’s it: from now on it opens like any app.

The AI helper needs your own key

Daftari comes with no AI key: you use your own, from the company you choose, and it is stored encrypted on your computer. The first-time setup walks you through it with step-by-step guides. The official pages:

  • Gemini (Google): has a free plan.
  • Groq: has a free plan.
  • OpenRouter: some free models with low limits.
  • OpenAI: paid.
  • Anthropic (Claude): paid.
  • Ollama: runs on your own computer, free, nothing leaves it.

What happens to your data

  • Your AI keys are encrypted with your computer’s own secure storage and kept in Daftari’s settings, never in your notes folder.
  • Lecture audio is kept in Daftari’s own folder, outside your notes, and is never synced.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, in the app or on this website. The website has no cookies and no forms, loads nothing from other sites, and its one small script stores nothing.
  • The check for a new version can be switched off in Settings → About.

Questions

Which Mac do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 or newer) and macOS 12 or later. To check: Apple menu → About This Mac → Chip. If it says “Intel”, that version isn’t ready yet.

What does my computer need?
  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or a Mac with Apple silicon.
  • About 400 MB of space; speech-to-text adds 150 MB to 1.6 GB, only if you want it.
  • A Mac with 8 GB works well with the Small and Medium speech models.
Is there a version for Intel Macs or Linux?

Not yet. Both are coming.

How do I check my download is genuine?

Compare its SHA-256 with the one published here (also in SHA256SUMS.txt):

  • Windows: 831b1dfc2b83d7757256852b7b325c213c10cd45e6defe7c76770ac62881108b
  • Mac: 427a038fd89c6d27cda778bdda87272ccf3d634856715ef6200b90817044f1d0

Windows (PowerShell): Get-FileHash Daftari-Windows-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256
Mac (Terminal): shasum -a 256 Daftari-Mac-AppleSilicon.dmg

How do updates work?

On Windows, Daftari downloads new versions itself and installs them when you close it. On a Mac it tells you when a new version is ready and opens this page.

Does the AI see my notes?

Only the text you ask it about, sent to the AI company you chose. Some free plans may use what you send to improve their products, so keep private notes out of free-plan requests. Ollama runs on your own computer, so nothing leaves it.

When does Daftari use the internet?

Only when you ask it to: your AI company, GitHub (if you turn on sync, and to look for a new version) and downloading the speech-to-text program. Recording and speech-to-text work offline.

Which languages does it understand?

Speech-to-text understands English for now. You can write notes in any language.

What else can it do?

Nested pages, tables and code, search through every word, maths typed as LaTeX, spell checking, a trash you can restore from, and optional sync through GitHub or a cloud folder.

Does it cost anything?

No. Daftari is free:

  • no account, no subscription, no adverts;
  • the only possible cost is an AI provider, if you choose a paid one. Some have a free plan, and Daftari works fully without one.
Why does Windows or macOS warn me?

Because Daftari is not registered with Microsoft or Apple. Registering means paying them every year so that your computer recognises who made a program, and Daftari, a free project by one person, is not registered. The warning appears when you first open it:

  • Windows: “Windows protected your PC” → More info → Run anyway.
  • Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (Apple’s guide).

Step by step: Opening it the first time.

Is it safe?
  • Checkable downloads: the installers are built automatically on GitHub and published with a SHA-256 checksum, so you can check your file is the one that was published.
  • Only what it needs: Daftari reads and writes your notes folder, its own settings folder and files you pick yourself, and uses the microphone only while you record.
  • Little internet: it goes online only for the things listed under How it looks after your data.
  • Nothing runs by surprise: programs attached to a page are never opened, only shown in their folder.

Found a security problem? Report it privately.

Where do my notes go?

Into a folder you choose the first time Daftari opens:

  • Only on this computer: “Documents/Daftari Notes”;
  • In a cloud folder: OneDrive, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox;
  • Synced with GitHub, for advanced users.

Every page is its own file; pictures and attachments sit next to them in an assets folder. Recordings and Daftari’s settings are kept apart, in Daftari’s own folder on your computer.

Does it work offline?
  • Without the internet: writing, search, flashcards, quizzes, recording, and speech-to-text once its model is downloaded.
  • Needs a connection: an online AI provider (Ollama runs on your own computer instead), sync, downloads and updates.
How do I remove it?

Open Settings → General → Remove Daftari from this computer…

  • Choose whether Daftari’s settings, keys and recordings go too.
  • Your notes stay unless you tick them and type DELETE.
  • Everything goes to the Recycle Bin or Bin first, so it can be put back.
How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?
  1. Open Daftari’s Issues page on GitHub and choose New issue (you need a free account: how to make one).
  2. Say what you did, what happened, and your Daftari version (Settings → About).

A security problem? Don’t post it publicly: use Security → Report a vulnerability in the repository (GitHub’s guide).
