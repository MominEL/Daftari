A quiet notebook for long lectures.

Daftari (Arabic دفتري, “my notebook”) records a lecture, turns it into written notes, and keeps everything as plain files in a folder you choose on your own computer. No account, no subscription, no lock-in.

An example transcript

  1. 47:02So an inverse modulo n only exists when a and n share no factor.
  2. 47:18In other words, when the gcd of a and n is one.
  3. 47:41Euler’s theorem gives it to us directly: a to the phi of n is one, mod n.
  4. 48:05So the inverse is a to the phi of n minus one.

Modular inverses

  • An inverse of a modulo n exists only when gcd(a, n) = 1.
  • Euler’s theorem: aφ(n) ≡ 1 (mod n)
  • So a−1 ≡ aφ(n)−1 (mod n)
FlashcardWhen does a have an inverse modulo n?
AnswerExactly when gcd(a, n) = 1.

What it does

  • WriteHeadings, lists, tables, code, toggles, formulas, flashcards and quizzes. Everything else stays hidden until you ask.
  • RecordRecord a lecture or a call, watch it turn into text as it goes, and get structured notes at the end. The speech-to-text runs on your computer.
  • AskImprove, summarise or quiz yourself on a page. You always see a preview first, and nothing is replaced until you say so.
  • KeepPlain files you can back up, sync with the service you already use, or read without Daftari. Deleted pages go to a Trash you can restore from.
  • MathsType $…$ for an equation. Shorthand turns a/b into a fraction as you type, and templates cover the formulas you write all the time.
  • SpellingFixes clear typos as you type, and leaves code, formulas, acronyms and names alone.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or macOS 12 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
  • Disk: the Windows installer is 108 MB and Daftari takes about 380 MB once installed; the Mac download is 127 MB.
  • Speech-to-text (optional), downloaded inside Daftari when you want it: a speech model of 148 MB (Base), 488 MB (Small), 1.5 GB (Medium) or 1.6 GB (Large v3 Turbo). On Windows the speech program adds 9 MB, or 685 MB for computers with an NVIDIA graphics card. On a Mac it is installed with Homebrew, and Daftari shows the two commands.
  • Memory: a Mac with 8 GB works well with the Small and Medium models. Daftari suggests one for your computer.
  • Internet: only to download Daftari and the speech models, to sync, and to use an online AI provider. Writing, search, flashcards, recording and speech-to-text work offline.
Check that your download is genuine

Each file has a SHA-256 checksum above (and in SHA256SUMS.txt). After downloading, compare it with your own:

Windows (PowerShell): Get-FileHash Daftari-Windows-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256

Mac (Terminal): shasum -a 256 Daftari-Mac-AppleSilicon.dmg

If the two do not match exactly, do not open the file.

Opening it the first time

Because the installers are not signed, Windows and macOS warn you once. That is expected. Only continue if you downloaded the file from this page.

Windows

  1. Run the installer. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Follow the installer, then open Daftari from the Start menu.

Mac

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Daftari into Applications.
  2. Open Daftari. macOS will say it cannot verify it. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click Open Anyway next to Daftari. Enter your password if asked. (The button is there for about an hour after the first try.)

Updates: on Windows, Daftari downloads new versions itself and installs them when you close it. On a Mac it tells you a new version is ready and opens this page.

The AI helper needs your own key

Daftari comes with no AI key: you use your own, from the company you choose, and it is stored encrypted on your computer. The first-time setup walks you through it with step-by-step guides. The official pages:

  • Gemini (Google): has a free plan.
  • Groq: has a free plan.
  • OpenRouter: some free models with low limits.
  • OpenAI: paid.
  • Anthropic (Claude): paid.
  • Ollama: runs on your own computer, free, nothing leaves it.

Free tiers can be generous, but some providers may use what you send them to improve their products, so keep private notes out of free-tier requests. Daftari works fully without the AI.

How it looks after your data

  • Your AI keys are locked away. They are encrypted with your computer’s own secure storage and kept in Daftari’s settings, never in your notes folder.
  • Recordings stay on your computer. Lecture audio is kept in Daftari’s own folder, outside your notes, and is never synced.
  • No analytics, no tracking. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, in the app and on this website (no cookies, no forms, nothing from other sites; its one small script only moves the pictures as you scroll).
  • You decide about updates. The check for a new version can be switched off in Settings → About.

Daftari contacts the internet only when you ask it to: the AI company you set up, GitHub (if you turn on sync, and to look for a new version), and the download sites for the speech-to-text program. Your notes stay in the folder you chose; if you pick a cloud folder or turn on GitHub sync, that service holds a copy.

Questions

Short answers, grouped. Click a question to open it.

Getting started

Does it cost anything?

No. Daftari is free, with no account and no subscription. The only possible cost is the AI provider you choose: some have a free plan, some charge for what you use, and Daftari works fully without one.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), and Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 12 or later. Intel Macs and Linux are coming. There is no phone version. What you need lists the disk space.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me?

Because the installers are not signed. A signature is a paid certificate that tells your computer who made a program; Daftari is a free project by one person and does not have one yet. The warning appears once. Opening it the first time shows exactly what to click.

Is it safe?

The installers are built automatically on GitHub from the source code and published with a SHA-256 checksum, so you can check that your file is the one that was published. Daftari only reads and writes the notes folder you choose, its own settings folder and files you pick yourself, uses the microphone only while you record, and contacts the internet only for the things listed under How it looks after your data. Programs attached to a page are never run: Daftari only shows them in their folder.

Your notes

Where do my notes go?

Into a folder you choose the first time Daftari opens: “Documents/Daftari Notes”, a folder in OneDrive, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox, or one you sync with GitHub. Every page is its own file, and pictures and attachments sit next to them in an assets folder. Recordings and Daftari’s settings are kept separately, in Daftari’s own folder on your computer.

Can I use my notes on two computers?

Yes, two ways. Put the notes folder in a cloud folder (OneDrive, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox) on both computers, or turn on sync with your own private GitHub repository in Settings → Sync. With GitHub, if the same page was changed on both computers, Daftari keeps both versions. With a cloud folder, avoid editing the same page on two computers at once.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Writing, search, flashcards, quizzes, recording and speech-to-text (once the speech model is downloaded) all work without the internet. You need a connection for an online AI provider (Ollama runs on your own computer), for sync, and for downloads and updates.

Can I get my notes out of Daftari?

They are already ordinary files in your folder. Settings → Notes → Export all notes also makes a zip with one Markdown file per page, the pictures and attachments, in the same folders as in Daftari, so you can open them in almost any other notes app.

Lectures and AI

Do I need an AI key?

No. Writing, recording, transcription, search and flashcards all work without one. A key adds the AI helper and the written notes from a lecture; the first-time setup explains how to get one, and some providers have a free plan.

Which languages can it transcribe?

English, for now. The speech-to-text runs on your computer and is set to English for every model.

What happens to my lecture recordings?

The audio stays on your computer, in Daftari’s own folder (not your notes folder), and is never synced. After you save a lecture’s notes, Daftari offers to delete the audio and keep the transcript; you can also export a recording as an audio file or delete it from the Recordings list.

Updating, removing and help

How do I update?

Windows: Daftari downloads a new version by itself and installs it when you close the app, or straight away with Update now in Settings → About. It never updates in the middle of a recording or a transcription. Mac: Daftari tells you a new version is ready and opens this page; download it and drag it into Applications to replace the old one. Your notes are not touched either way.

How do I remove it?

From Daftari 0.2: Settings → General → Remove Daftari from this computer… You choose whether Daftari’s settings, keys and recordings go too, and whether your notes do (they are kept unless you tick them and type DELETE). Everything goes to the Recycle Bin or Bin first, so it can be put back.

In version 0.1: on Windows, Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Daftari → Uninstall; on a Mac, quit Daftari and drag it from Applications to the Bin. Your notes folder stays. To remove Daftari’s own folder too, delete %APPDATA%\Daftari on Windows or ~/Library/Application Support/Daftari on a Mac.

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Open an issue on Daftari’s GitHub page (you need a free GitHub account). Please say what you did, what happened, and your Daftari version (Settings → About). For a security problem, use the private report in the repository’s Security tab instead, so it is not public.
