A quiet notebook for long lectures.

Daftari (Arabic دفتري, “my notebook”) records a lecture, turns it into written notes, and keeps everything as plain files in a folder you choose on your own computer. No account, no subscription, no lock-in.

From the lecture hall to your notes.

Daftari records with your microphone, your computer’s own sound, or both. Speech-to-text runs on your computer while you listen, and the AI you choose turns what was said into notes before the lecture ends.

An example transcript

  1. 47:02So an inverse modulo n only exists when a and n share no factor.
  2. 47:18In other words, when the gcd of a and n is one.
  3. 47:41Euler’s theorem gives it to us directly: a to the phi of n is one, mod n.
  4. 48:05So the inverse is a to the phi of n minus one.

Modular inverses

  • An inverse of a modulo n exists only when gcd(a, n) = 1.
  • Euler’s theorem: aφ(n) ≡ 1 (mod n)
  • So a−1 ≡ aφ(n)−1 (mod n)
FlashcardWhen does a have an inverse modulo n?
AnswerExactly when gcd(a, n) = 1.

What it does

  • WriteHeadings, lists, tables, code, toggles, formulas, flashcards and quizzes. Everything else stays hidden until you ask.
  • RecordRecord a lecture or a call, watch it turn into text as it goes, and get structured notes at the end. The speech-to-text runs on your computer.
  • AskImprove, summarise or quiz yourself on a page. You always see a preview first, and nothing is replaced until you say so.
  • KeepPlain files you can back up, sync with the service you already use, or read without Daftari. Deleted pages go to a Trash you can restore from.
  • MathsType $…$ for an equation. Shorthand turns a/b into a fraction as you type, and templates cover the formulas you write all the time.
  • SpellingFixes clear typos as you type, and leaves code, formulas, acronyms and names alone.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or macOS 12 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
  • Disk: the Windows installer is 108 MB and Daftari takes about 380 MB once installed; the Mac download is 127 MB.
  • Speech-to-text (optional), downloaded inside Daftari when you want it: a speech model of 148 MB (Base), 488 MB (Small), 1.5 GB (Medium) or 1.6 GB (Large v3 Turbo). On Windows the speech program adds 9 MB, or 685 MB for computers with an NVIDIA graphics card. On a Mac it is installed with Homebrew, and Daftari shows the two commands.
  • Memory: a Mac with 8 GB works well with the Small and Medium models. Daftari suggests one for your computer.
  • Internet: only to download Daftari and the speech models, to sync, and to use an online AI provider. Writing, search, flashcards, recording and speech-to-text work offline.
Check that your download is genuine

Each file has a SHA-256 checksum above (and in SHA256SUMS.txt). After downloading, compare it with your own:

Windows (PowerShell): Get-FileHash Daftari-Windows-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256

Mac (Terminal): shasum -a 256 Daftari-Mac-AppleSilicon.dmg

If the two do not match exactly, do not open the file.

Opening it the first time

Because the installers are not signed, Windows and macOS warn you once. That is expected. Only continue if you downloaded the file from this page.

Windows

  1. Run the installer. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Follow the installer, then open Daftari from the Start menu.

Mac

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Daftari into Applications.
  2. Open Daftari. macOS says “Daftari Not Opened” because it could not verify it. That is expected (Daftari is free and not registered with Apple). Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click Open Anyway next to Daftari. (The button is there for about an hour after the first try.)
  4. The box appears once more: click Open Anyway and enter your Mac password. That's all. From now on Daftari opens like any other app.

The same steps are shown in the window that opens with the download. Each new version you download asks once again.

Updates: on Windows, Daftari downloads new versions itself and installs them when you close it. On a Mac it tells you a new version is ready and opens this page.

The AI helper needs your own key

Daftari comes with no AI key: you use your own, from the company you choose, and it is stored encrypted on your computer. The first-time setup walks you through it with step-by-step guides. The official pages:

  • Gemini (Google): has a free plan.
  • Groq: has a free plan.
  • OpenRouter: some free models with low limits.
  • OpenAI: paid.
  • Anthropic (Claude): paid.
  • Ollama: runs on your own computer, free, nothing leaves it.

Free tiers can be generous, but some providers may use what you send them to improve their products, so keep private notes out of free-tier requests. Daftari works fully without the AI.

How it looks after your data

  • Your AI keys are locked away. They are encrypted with your computer’s own secure storage and kept in Daftari’s settings, never in your notes folder.
  • Recordings stay on your computer. Lecture audio is kept in Daftari’s own folder, outside your notes, and is never synced.
  • No analytics, no tracking. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, in the app and on this website (no cookies, no forms, nothing from other sites; its one small script only moves the pictures and the lamp, and switches between light and dark; it stores nothing).
  • You decide about updates. The check for a new version can be switched off in Settings → About.

Daftari contacts the internet only when you ask it to: the AI company you set up, GitHub (if you turn on sync, and to look for a new version), and the download sites for the speech-to-text program. Your notes stay in the folder you chose; if you pick a cloud folder or turn on GitHub sync, that service holds a copy.

Questions

Short answers, grouped. Click a question to open it.

Getting started

Does it cost anything?

No. Daftari is free:

  • no account, no subscription, no adverts;
  • the only possible cost is an AI provider, if you choose a paid one. Some have a free plan, and Daftari works fully without one.
Which computers does it run on?
  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 12 or later. Not sure? See Apple’s list of Macs with Apple silicon.
  • Intel Macs and Linux are coming. There is no phone version.

What you need lists the disk space.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me?

Because the installers are not signed. A signature is a paid certificate that tells your computer who made a program, and Daftari, a free project by one person, does not have one yet. The warning appears once:

  • Windows: “Windows protected your PC” → More info → Run anyway.
  • Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (Apple’s guide).

Step by step: Opening it the first time.

Is it safe?
  • Checkable downloads: the installers are built automatically on GitHub and published with a SHA-256 checksum, so you can check your file is the one that was published.
  • Only what it needs: Daftari reads and writes your notes folder, its own settings folder and files you pick yourself, and uses the microphone only while you record.
  • Little internet: it goes online only for the things listed under How it looks after your data.
  • Nothing runs by surprise: programs attached to a page are never opened, only shown in their folder.

Found a security problem? Report it privately.

Your notes

Where do my notes go?

Into a folder you choose the first time Daftari opens:

  • Only on this computer: “Documents/Daftari Notes”;
  • In a cloud folder: OneDrive, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox;
  • Synced with GitHub, for advanced users.

Every page is its own file; pictures and attachments sit next to them in an assets folder. Recordings and Daftari’s settings are kept apart, in Daftari’s own folder on your computer.

Can I use my notes on two computers?

Yes, in one of two ways:

  • A cloud folder (OneDrive, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox) that both computers can see. Avoid editing the same page on both at once.
  • GitHub sync, in Settings → Sync, with your own private repository (how to make one). If a page changed on both computers, Daftari keeps both versions.

Use one or the other, not both together.

Does it work offline?
  • Without the internet: writing, search, flashcards, quizzes, recording, and speech-to-text once its model is downloaded.
  • Needs a connection: an online AI provider (Ollama runs on your own computer instead), sync, downloads and updates.
Can I get my notes out of Daftari?
  • They are already ordinary files in your folder.
  • Settings → Notes → Export all notes makes a zip with one Markdown file per page, plus the pictures and attachments, in the same folders as in Daftari. Almost any other notes app can open them.

Lectures and AI

Do I need an AI key?

No. Writing, recording, transcription, search and flashcards all work without one. A key adds:

  • the AI helper (improve, summarise, make flashcards or a quiz from a page);
  • written notes from a lecture, made as it goes.

The first-time setup shows how to get one. Which providers, and which are free.

Which languages can it transcribe?

English, for now. The speech-to-text (whisper.cpp) runs on your computer and is set to English for every model.

What happens to my lecture recordings?
  • The audio stays on your computer, in Daftari’s own folder (not your notes folder), and is never synced.
  • After you save a lecture’s notes, Daftari offers to delete the audio and keep the transcript.
  • From the Recordings list you can export a recording as an audio file, or delete it.

Updating, removing and help

How do I update?
  • Windows: Daftari downloads a new version by itself and installs it when you close it, or at once with Update now in Settings → About. Never in the middle of a recording or a transcription.
  • Mac: Daftari tells you a new version is ready and opens this page. Download it and drag it into Applications to replace the old one.

Your notes are not touched either way. What changed in each version.

How do I remove it?

From Daftari 0.2: Settings → General → Remove Daftari from this computer…

  • Choose whether Daftari’s settings, keys and recordings go too.
  • Your notes stay unless you tick them and type DELETE.
  • Everything goes to the Recycle Bin or Bin first, so it can be put back.

In version 0.1:

  • Windows: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Daftari → Uninstall (Microsoft’s guide).
  • Mac: quit Daftari and drag it from Applications to the Bin.
  • Your notes folder stays. To remove Daftari’s own folder too, delete %APPDATA%\Daftari (Windows) or ~/Library/Application Support/Daftari (Mac).
How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?
  1. Open Daftari’s Issues page on GitHub and choose New issue (you need a free account: how to make one).
  2. Say what you did, what happened, and your Daftari version (Settings → About).

A security problem? Don’t post it publicly: use Security → Report a vulnerability in the repository (GitHub’s guide).
