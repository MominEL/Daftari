A notebook for lectures, revision and ideas

A quiet notebook for long lectures.

Daftari (Arabic دفتري, “my notebook”) is one calm page, a command bar, and an AI helper only when you ask. It can record a lecture and turn it into written notes, all on your own computer.

Your notes are plain files in a folder you choose. No account, no subscription, no lock-in.

From lecture to notes

Press record. Listen. Read your notes afterwards.

  1. 1

    Record

    Use the microphone, your computer’s own sound (an online lecture or a call), or both. Audio is saved every 30 seconds, so a crash never loses the lecture.

  2. 2

    It becomes text

    Speech-to-text runs on your computer while you listen. Nothing is uploaded, and you can still read the transcript as it grows.

  3. 3

    Notes appear

    Every few minutes the AI you chose writes structured notes. At the end you get a clean page and a transcript page, and you decide what to keep.

What it does

Everything a student needs, nothing in the way.

Write

Headings, lists, tables, code, toggles, formulas, flashcards and quizzes. Tabs for the pages you have open; everything else stays hidden until you ask.

Record

Record a lecture (or a call), see it turn into text as it goes, and get structured notes at the end. The speech-to-text runs on your computer.

Ask

Improve, summarise or quiz yourself on a page. You always see a preview first, and nothing is replaced until you say so.

Keep

Plain files you can back up, sync with the service you already use, or read without Daftari. Deleted pages go to a Trash you can restore from.

Maths

Type $…$ for an equation, with shorthand that turns a/b into a fraction as you type, a symbol keyboard, and templates for the formulas you write all the time.

Calm

A dark, warm page that is easy on the eyes in a lecture hall. No pop-ups, no sound, no “what’s new”. Fixes obvious typos as you type and never touches code, formulas or names.

Check that your download is genuine

Each file has a SHA-256 checksum above (and in SHA256SUMS.txt). After downloading, compare it with your own:

Windows (PowerShell): Get-FileHash Daftari-Windows-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256

Mac (Terminal): shasum -a 256 Daftari-Mac-AppleSilicon.dmg

If the two do not match exactly, do not open the file.

First launch

Opening it the first time

Because the installers are not signed, Windows and macOS warn you once. That is expected. Only continue if you downloaded the file from this page.

Windows

  1. Run the installer. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Follow the installer, then open Daftari from the Start menu.

Mac

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Daftari into Applications.
  2. Open Daftari. macOS will say it cannot verify it. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click Open Anyway next to Daftari. Enter your password if asked. (The button is there for about an hour after the first try.)

Updates: on Windows, Daftari downloads new versions itself and installs them when you close it. On a Mac it tells you a new version is ready and opens this page.

Optional

The AI helper needs your own key

Daftari comes with no AI key: you use your own, from the company you choose, and it is stored encrypted on your computer. The first-time setup walks you through it with step-by-step guides. The official pages:

  • Gemini (Google): has a free plan.
  • Groq: has a free plan.
  • OpenRouter: some free models with low limits.
  • OpenAI: paid.
  • Anthropic (Claude): paid.
  • Ollama: runs on your own computer, free, nothing leaves it.

Free tiers can be generous, but some providers may use what you send them to improve their products, so keep private notes out of free-tier requests. Daftari works fully without the AI.

Privacy

Your notes stay yours.

  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising. This website has none either: no scripts, no cookies, no forms.
  • Your notes stay in the folder you chose. If you pick a cloud folder or turn on GitHub sync, that service holds a copy: that is your choice.
  • Recordings and transcripts stay on your computer and are never synced.
  • Daftari contacts the internet only when you ask it to: the AI company you set up, GitHub (if you turn on sync), the download sites for the speech-to-text program, and GitHub to look for a new version of Daftari (you can switch that off in Settings → About).
