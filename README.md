# Daftari

A free notes app for students. Record a lecture, get the notes, revise from them.

**[Download from the website](https://mominel.github.io/Daftari/)** · Windows 10/11 · Macs with Apple silicon

- Records your lecture and turns it into text on your own computer.
- The AI writes notes as you go. You check them before saving.
- Flashcards and quizzes from your notes.
- Every page is a plain file in a folder you choose.

## First time opening it

Daftari isn't registered with Microsoft or Apple, so your computer asks once.

- **Windows:** More info → Run anyway.
- **Mac:** open it from Applications, click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

## Privacy

Your notes stay in your folder. Recordings never leave your computer. No accounts, no tracking.
